Intentio

Years of piano lessons taught me that the hardest feedback to get is on interpretation — a teacher can hear when a phrase feels rushed or a dynamic doesn't land, but that kind of listening is rare and expensive outside the lesson room. Intentio is my attempt to make that closer listening more available: upload a recording of yourself playing a piece, and it reads your performance against the score, surfacing tempo, dynamics, and phrasing choices as a reference point for your own interpretation — never a verdict.
