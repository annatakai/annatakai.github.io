Intentio

My piano teacher instilled in me that playing a piece well is not enough — a classical pianist has to interpret the composer, reading into the specificity and precision behind every marking on the score to understand what they actually intended. That kind of close listening is rare and expensive outside the lesson room. Intentio is my attempt to make it more available: upload a recording of yourself playing a piece, and it reads your performance against the score, giving pianists feedback on how close their interpretation comes to what the composer aimed for — a reference point, never a verdict.
