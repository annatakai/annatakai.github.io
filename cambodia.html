education is a human right. every child deserves a quality education.

these ideas motivated me to establish for every child an education, a global community that has expanded to 80 youth from seven countries increasing accessibility to quality education for children and teachers.

for every child an education worked with the cambodian government to enhance their english curriculum workbooks for grades four to six.

eventually, we developed and published workbooks for three grade levels, each 150 pages long, together. our workbooks are now in the hands of 2,000 students. this wouldn't have been possible without my community uniting through our shared motto: "leave no one behind."

my work in cambodia also led me to examine the systemic barriers that prevent children from accessing and completing their education. since 2022, i have been researching the causes of school dropout in rural cambodia under the mentorship of professor taku yamaguchi of the university of tsukuba.

research title:
school dropout rates in rural cambodia: political, economic and environmental barriers to education policy reform

my research has been peer-reviewed and presented at international academic conferences, including:
