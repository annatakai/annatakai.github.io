Mentaur

Growing up in Japan and helping care for my grandmother, I saw how an aging population affects not only families, but entire communities. As I thought about more people retiring, I became interested in what happens to the knowledge and judgment they have built over decades. Mentaur came from a personal question: how can we use AI not to replace people, but to preserve what they know and pass it on to the next generation?
