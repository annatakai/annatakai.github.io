ClearPath

ClearPath is an AI-powered platform designed to improve the quality and speed of international clinical trial drug shipments by addressing country-specific labeling regulations and global logistics disruptions, including natural disasters and geopolitical events. By integrating Chalk, Photon, and Grok, ClearPath combines three core features: an intelligent planner that visualizes shipment schedules and proposes adjustments, an automated communication system that notifies stakeholders of regulatory and logistical changes through iMessage, and a visual regulatory database that maps drug movements worldwide. Together, these features streamline planning, communication, and decision-making to help ensure that critical treatments reach patients safely and on time.
